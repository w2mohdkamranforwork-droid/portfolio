9278.ai — Human-Sounding AI Voice Agents

A global AI voice agent platform that answers inbound calls and runs outbound campaigns on a business's existing phone carrier, with conversations fast and natural enough to sound human.

Voice AIWebRTCSIPRAG

Working Purpose

Businesses lose leads every time a call goes unanswered or lands in a slow, robotic IVR. 9278.ai puts an AI voice agent on the line that picks up every call, 24/7, and holds a natural conversation: answering questions, qualifying leads and booking appointments.

It plugs into the carrier account a business already has, so there is no number porting. A native audio-to-audio engine keeps replies under 300ms, and agents answer from the business's own documents through a live-synced knowledge base.

Tech Stack

Audio-Native Voice Engine

Direct audio-to-audio modeling that keeps response latency under 300ms.

WebRTC / SIP / PSTN

Carrier-agnostic telephony on a distributed media network, with no number porting.

RAG Knowledge Base

Retrieval-augmented answers grounded in live-synced business documents.

Call Analytics

Real-time transcripts with sentiment and intent analysis on every call.

  • → 24/7 inbound virtual receptionist that never misses a call.

  • → Multilingual conversations with automatic language detection.
