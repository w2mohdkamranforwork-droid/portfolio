9278.io — AI Voice Agents for India

An AI voice receptionist platform built for Indian businesses: it answers calls in 10+ Indian languages, qualifies leads and books appointments around the clock, and is fully TRAI compliant.

Voice AIWebRTCSIPMultilingual

Working Purpose

Indian businesses field calls in many languages, and traditional IVR menus are slow and frustrating. 9278.io replaces them with AI voice agents that speak the caller's language, handle natural turn-taking and respond in under 300ms.

It runs on Indian media networks with Indian DID numbers, bills per second with no contracts, and ships with templates for sectors like BPO, BFSI, real estate and e-commerce so teams can go live quickly.

Tech Stack

Multilingual Voice AI

Natural conversations across 10+ Indian languages with fluid turn-taking.

WebRTC / SIP Trunking

Low-latency audio on Indian media networks, with Indian DID provisioning and number porting.

Transcription & Analytics

Call recording, real-time transcripts and analytics in a cloud dashboard.

TRAI Compliance

Built to meet Indian telecom regulations for business calling.

Key Features

  • → Handles inbound calls 24/7: qualifies leads and books appointments.
  • → Around 94ms responses, versus roughly 2400ms on a traditional IVR.
  • → Call scheduling, forwarding and high concurrency.
  • → Pre-built industry templates for BPO, BFSI, real estate, e-commerce and more.
  • → Prompt changes take effect instantly from the dashboard.
  • → Per-second billing with no long-term contracts.
